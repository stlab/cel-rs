# SheetHandle reentrant outcomes require borrow-free FIFO delivery

SheetHandle host callbacks must run after all enclosing owner borrows end, including rejection outcomes generated by reentry from accepted-state closures and model factories. Immediate emission on RefCell contention violates this contract even if the nested call returns ReentrantMutation. HandleState queues outcomes FIFO; emit enqueues, drain verifies and releases exclusive owner access and releases the queue borrow before invoking the host. A guard serializes callback reentry and resets on unwinding. Every owner-borrowing entry point drains after its local borrow ends. Preserve this invariant when adding entry points; callbacks may inspect or mutate the handle.
